How the plots and problem data were made

Every curve is calculated from the equilibrium m M + n L ⇌ MmLn with the mass balances in Section 2, solved numerically for each mixture. Constants used: Fe(II)–phen, log β3 = 21.3 and ε510 = 1.11 × 104; hydroxymethylferrocene–β-CD, K = 478 M−1; Fe(III)–SCN−, K = 1.4 × 102 M−1 and ε447 = 4.7 × 103, values of the size reported for this ion; Ni(Hdmg)2, 288.9 g mol−1 with complete precipitation; Cu(I)–BCA, log β2 = 17.2 and ε562 = 7.7 × 103. Small random errors (a few tenths of a milligram, or ±0.003 absorbance) were added to the problem data.
